## Tracking findings

For each audited requirement, record the production path, named source regression
and observed result in the owning child. If a substantial implementation gap
emerges, create a numbered child under that audit, such as 03.4.2.1. Give it
the source case, expected behavior and regression needed for closure. Link it
immediately and keep the affected requirement open. Other verified items can
be checked off independently.

Follow the [completion workflow](README.md#completing-and-moving-a-todo) for
each child. Move it to `complete/` on main when validated and update its row
here and in the index. Close 03.4 and 03 together once all three children and
all 03 requirements have evidence. TODOs 04–06 cover synchronization lifetimes,
general summaries and protocols. TODOs 07–08 verify the global byte bound,
including the mutex scratch reduction from `n * b` to `b`.
