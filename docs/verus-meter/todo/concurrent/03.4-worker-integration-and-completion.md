assigned to 03 is connected to actual executable bodies and verified transitions.
There are no body-entry/completion placeholders deferred to 05. Required rejection
paths remain sound, and 04–07 can consume the checked effects without reconstructing
worker lifetimes from scalar bounds or manually supplied ledger state.
