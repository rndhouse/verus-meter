# 03.4.2.6. Extract the inner Arc from a unique worker result

The [ownership audit](03.4.2-audit-loops-and-shared-ownership.md) found
that a joined `Arc<Arc<Vec<u8>>>` could be dropped, but taking its payload
with `Arc::into_inner` failed the uniqueness check at `2cf864351`. The worker
had returned the only outer handle. The [operation matrix](../../../concurrent-model.md#supported-operation-matrix)
covers extraction once that ownership is established.

- [x] Carry the direct inner handle's identity from the executable constructor
  through the selected worker's publication and join.
- [x] Prove exclusive ownership of the outer allocation before extraction.
  Release its control block and inline payload space and transfer ownership
  of the surviving inner allocation. Preserve any existing canonical backing.
- [x] Verify exact local bounds and one-byte failures across distinct visits.
  Reject extraction while a clone of the outer allocation is live.

## Evidence

Feature commit `fc48a9f79` extends the existing publication record and the
checked ownership transfer. The producer records which inner handle it moved
into the wrapper. The join reads that record from the selected collection slot.
Extraction separates the wrapper's checked layout from the bytes still owned
by the inner handle.

`unique_wrapper_releases_only_its_own_backing_at_extraction` verifies 97 live
bytes before extraction, 73 with the 19-byte buffer still owned, and 6 after
its drop. It rejects 72 and the nonunique outer. In
`joining_restores_the_capacity_from_the_selected_visit`, the earlier worker's
7-byte buffer accounts for 61 live bytes after extraction, and 60 fails.
`extracted_alias_keeps_the_original_shared_backing` checks the other ownership
case. A publisher alias and the extracted handle share one existing allocation,
giving 79 bytes and rejecting 78. All three regressions exercise the normal
verifier with its global concurrent-peak diagnostic enabled.
